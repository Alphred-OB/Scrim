Captions and AI

Captions from your voice. Edit by editing words.

Scrim listens to your recording, writes the words, times every one, and lets you cut the video by deleting text. Use your own AI key for speed, or keep it all on your PC.

01 · Generate

One click. Every word timed.

  1. 1Record with your mic on. Scrim keeps your voice on its own track, so it reads clean.
  2. 2Open Captions and press Generate. Words appear with the exact moment you said them.
  3. 3Pick a style. One or two lines, size, position, colors. It updates live in the preview.

Works for any language Whisper understands. Export the captions as a subtitle file too.

04 · AI keys

Bring your key. Or bring nothing.

Scrim has no AI subscription and no account. For fast, accurate captions you can paste a free Groq key. Prefer privacy? Choose This PC and Whisper runs offline.

  1. 1Get a free key at console.groq.com, under API Keys.
  2. 2In Scrim open Settings, then AI, and paste it in the Groq API key box.
  3. 3Leave it on Automatic. Groq is used, and your PC takes over if Groq is unreachable.

Your key is stored only on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose Groq, and then only the audio to be transcribed.
