What your video shows

How it works

Write it. Press record.

  1. 1Open Script on the home screen and type or paste what you want to say.
  2. 2Or import a .txt file. Lines that start with # become section titles.
  3. 3Start recording. The script appears and scrolls by itself, and waits when you pause.

Made for reading

Every control you would want.

Auto scroll, or not

Let it scroll by itself while you record, or keep it still and start it yourself.

Your speed

Ten speeds, from a calm read to fast. Change it while you speak.

Hotkeys

Alt+Shift+Space starts or stops it. Alt+Shift+Up and Down change the speed. Alt+Shift+Home jumps to the top.

Sections and size

# titles make parts easy to find. Text from 20 to 64 pixels, and the window opacity is yours to set.

Import a .txt

Bring a script you wrote elsewhere. A word count and read time tell you how long it runs.

Hidden from capture

The window is excluded from the screen capture by Windows, so it cannot leak into your video or a screen share.
